Secondary fault information is partly recoverable: the latent probe identifies 20/30 CPU-containing combinations, versus 0/30 for Dreamer's original reward path. However, it regresses to 5/10 on CPU-only incidents and remains below the raw probe. This implicates the diagnosis path without proving the latent representation is sufficient for reliable triage. All probes fit their 140 training targets perfectly, including shuffled labels: the 640-dimensional latent probe has enough capacity to memorize noise. The next step is a regularized multi-label diagnostic head selected using training-only cross-validation, followed by a new held-out batch. These results do not justify retraining the entire world model or promoting this probe yet.
